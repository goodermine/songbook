# Iconoodle doodles — attribution

The charcoal line-art PNGs in this folder are rasterised from hand-drawn SVG
doodles in **Iconoodle** by **Nitish (NK2552003)**.

- Source: https://github.com/NK2552003/Iconoodle
- Licence: **MIT** — free for commercial use, no warranty. Attribution is not
  required by the licence, but is appreciated.

Only Iconoodle's own hand-drawn doodle collections were used
(`fruit-vegetable`, `educational`, `fast-food`, `nature`); the aggregated
third-party icon sets in that repo (`public-*`, etc.) were **not** vendored.

Processing: each SVG was rendered to PNG, its baked white background removed,
recoloured to the engine's charcoal ink (`#262B2E`), trimmed to content and
downscaled to 800 px on the long edge. Filled/duplicate variants were filtered
out to keep clean line-art (nature silhouettes kept intentionally solid).
